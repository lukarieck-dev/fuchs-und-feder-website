3. Our Pinterest app

Fuchs und Feder uses its own app with the Pinterest API to create boards and publish Pins about our teaching materials on our own Pinterest business account. A person reviews and approves every single Pin before it is created.

The app only processes data of our own account: the access token Pinterest issues after we authorize the app, the account name and account type, and our own boards and Pins. This data is stored on our own server and is not shared with anyone. The app does not access or store data of other Pinterest users. When we disconnect the app, we delete the access token. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing our materials on Pinterest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you view, save or click our Pins on Pinterest, the Pinterest Privacy Policy applies.
